Now that you've got a solid understanding of General's current network state, it's time to start sketching out your master plan.

Based on your assessment findings and General's stated requirements, outline the key components of your proposed network update.

For each component, provide a high-level rationale.How will this help address General's current challenges or enable their future objectives?

At this stage, focus on the "what" and the "why". We'll get into the nitty-gritty of "how" later on. The goal here is to paint a clear, compelling picture of your vision for General's network transformation.

Remember, a good plan is like a good network: structured, efficient, and able to handle whatever comes its way!
