## Lab requirements

The following requirements describe the necessary deliverables for the Network Update Lab.

| Criteria                                                                                                                                            | Points |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Your name, the slide deck's title, and the topic are present on an introductory slide.                                                              | 2      |
| The slide deck was shared publicly so that it can be viewed by the instructor on their first attempt when grading.                                  | 2      |
| The slide deck contains an audio and video presentation of the slide deck's main content.                                                           | 30     |
| The slide deck avoids unexplained jargon and acronyms.                                                                                              | 2      |
| The slide deck's content supports the verbal presentation instead of being a script.                                                                | 4      |
| The presentation is delivered within the time limit provided.                                                                                       | 4      |
| Visual elements and design in the slide deck are professional.                                                                                      | 5      |
| The presentation promotes engagement from the audience through the use of visuals, storytelling, confidence, anecdotes, or anticipating objections. | 4      |
| The presentation ends with a clear call to action and next steps.                                                                                   | 2      |
| The slide deck includes a link to the full business proposal document.                                                                              | 10     |
| The business proposal document was shared publicly so that it can be viewed by the instructor on their first attempt when grading.                  | 2      |
| The business proposal document contains an **Executive summary** section.                                                                           | 4      |
| The business proposal document contains a **Current state assessment** section.                                                                     | 4      |
| The business proposal document contains a **Proposed network update plan** section.                                                                 | 4      |
| The business proposal document contains a **Cost-benefit analysis** section.                                                                        | 4      |
| The business proposal document contains an **Implementation plan** section.                                                                         | 4      |
| The business proposal document contains a **Conclusion and next steps** section.                                                                    | 4      |
| The business proposal document is formatted in a clear manner                                                                                       | 4      |
| The business proposal document uses clear and professional language.                                                                                | 5      |
| **Total possible points**                                                                                                                           | 100    |
